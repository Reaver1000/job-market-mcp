# Example prompts

These work once the `job-market` MCP server is connected.

- How many live postings are in the dataset, and what share is remote?
- Which companies have the most live postings right now?
- Find implementation consultant roles that are actually remote.
- Show me junior data analyst roles posted in the last week.
- What are the ten oldest live postings?
- How many junior-titled ads state no experience bar at all?
- Which skills appear most often in junior ads?
- How often is pay disclosed for junior roles compared with senior roles?
- What has Cloudflare posted over the last month?
- Reconstruct the market as it looked on 2026-11-21.
